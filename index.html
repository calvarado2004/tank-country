Mission brief

The Crimson Corps has fortified the ridgeline above the valley. Their farms are supply dumps. Their outposts are gun nests. You have one tank, a rifle squad, and Battery One on call.

Flatten the barns. Break their armor and their infantry. Take Alpha, Bravo, and Charlie before the next bomber wave. The valley is counting on you.

Pocket field manual

How to win the ridge.

Drive & shoot

WASD drives in camera space. The hull turns toward your heading. The turret follows the mouse independently. LMB fires a high-velocity shell that dents the earth and shatters structures. Space sprays the coaxial MG at infantry.

Command the squad

RMB sends Able squad to a point. G calls them back to the tank. The squad follows, auto-engages, and can hold a capture ring without the tank. Enemy rifle squads defend every outpost — kill them or keep them out of the ring.

Call fire missions

F arms Battery One, then click a target. The battery fires three high-arc shells after a short delay. It needs a spotter with line of sight: the tank or a living rifleman. Targets too close to the tank are refused. Each mission costs one of three shells; captures resupply the battery.

Capture outposts

Hold the marked ring with the tank or the squad, and clear hostile armor and infantry from it. Capture all three ridgeline outposts to win. Each capture patches your hull, resupplies the squad, and adds a shell to Battery One.

Know your enemy

Tanks close in and trade shots. Artillery lobs marked, high-arc shells — keep moving. Bombers drop sticks of bombs; aim at the aircraft to lock the main gun, or let captured flak guns help.

Use the ground

Craters change the terrain. Barns and fences come apart under fire. The ridge road is the fastest route, but the high ground is exposed.
